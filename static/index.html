      Keyboard shortcuts

      Screening (the video keys)

      Click
      Pause, mark a point, and open the wheel
      Drag
      Pause, draw a box, and open the wheel
      Click a mark
      Paused or playing: change its species
      Space
      Play or pause
      ← →
      Hold to rewind or fast forward at 4x; let go to stop
      , .
      Step one frame (1/30 s)
      [ ]
      Slower or faster, 0.5x to 3x
      Home
      Back to the start
      F
      Full screen on or off
      P
      Show or hide the species keys panel
      G
      Show or hide the grid and center line
      Z
      Undo the last pick: discard an unsaved one, else remove the last sighting
      D
      Mark the video done, or reopen it
      N
      Open the next video in the list
      ?
      Open or close this card

      Marking (the species wheel)

      Click a slice
      Save that species; the video stays paused
      1 to 9, 0
      Save the pinned species; the video stays paused
      A to Z
      Filter the wheel; ⌫ edits
      ↑ ↓, scroll
      Spin the outer ring, or move through the matches
      Enter
      Save the highlighted match; the video stays paused
      Space, the hub
      Later: save without a species. On a saved mark: Unlabel or Keep. While you type a filter, Space types a space
      ⌫ twice
      On a saved mark: delete it with its images and track. The first press turns the hub red; any other key cancels
      Shift + a pick
      Save and play on
      , ., ← →
      Step one frame; the mark and the wheel stay
      Esc, right-click
      Cancel the mark and play on
      Click the picture
      Move the mark there
      Click elsewhere
      Cancel the mark; the video stays paused
      Tab, Shift+Tab
      Walk the highlight around the wheel; an open guide follows it. The note box takes a click.
